How an investigation moves

Every assignment follows the same disciplined sequence, so agencies know what to expect and when.

  1. Plan

    Review the accepted claims and build an investigative plan that identifies witnesses, evidence, and questions for each claim.

  2. Gather

    Obtain sworn testimony and documentary evidence from the complainant, management officials, and witnesses.

  3. Analyze

    Organize the record by claim and basis so each element is supported by the evidence collected.

  4. Deliver

    Produce a complete, formatted report of investigation, reviewed against the accepted claims before it reaches the agency.
