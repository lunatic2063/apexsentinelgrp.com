About Apex Sentinel Group

A veteran-owned professional services firm focused on fair, thorough, and timely resolution of workplace complaints.

Why the firm exists

Federal agencies face steady complaint volume, strict regulatory timelines, and records that must hold up on review. Apex Sentinel Group was established in Lehigh Valley, Pennsylvania in 2026 to give agencies and prime contractors a dependable partner for that work.

The firm pairs a working command of federal EEO regulations and procedures with disciplined investigative methods, so records are complete, impartial, and delivered on schedule. Separately, the firm advises organizations on artificial intelligence and automation in their own operations.

Who we serve

Apex Sentinel Group supports federal agency EEO and human resources offices, prime contractors that need qualified subcontract support, and organizations that want to strengthen their complaint and compliance programs.

At a glance

  • Veteran-owned small business
  • EEO investigators with 10+ years of experience
  • Attorneys holding Juris Doctor degrees
  • Established 2026 in Pennsylvania
  • Work performed under 29 C.F.R. Part 1614 and EEO MD-110
  • EFT and Government Purchase Card accepted

Our team

Experienced practitioners who know the federal complaint process from the inside.

  • Seasoned EEO investigators. Multiple investigators, each with more than 10 years of experience conducting federal EEO investigations.
  • Attorneys on the team. Attorneys holding Juris Doctor degrees bring legal analysis to complex and sensitive matters.
  • Federal EEO program experience. Team members understand how complaints move through agency EEO programs, from informal counseling through investigation.

What sets the work apart

  • Grounded in the federal process. Work reflects how complaints move through intake, counseling, acceptance, and investigation.
  • Investigative discipline. Rigor in evidence gathering and discretion in sensitive matters.
  • Quality control on every product. Each work product is reviewed against the accepted claims and agency requirements before it is delivered.
  • A separate, responsible AI practice. Advisory work on artificial intelligence and automation is offered as its own consulting service, with governance and human oversight built into every recommendation.
  • Consistent, agency-ready formatting. Work products follow agency standards so reviewers spend their time on substance.
  • Responsive turnaround. Schedules are built around regulatory deadlines.
